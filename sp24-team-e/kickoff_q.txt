What is the project focus/overall goal?     

The project focus/overall goal is to identify new locations for water bottle filling stations across the Charles River 
Campus of Boston University based on multiple factors, including the presence of air conditioning in buildings, foot 
traffic determined through Wifi data, proximity to alternative water sources, and more if needed.

Why is this project important?    

This project holds significant value since Boston University has been transitioning from traditional drinking fountains 
to combination fountains-bottle filters. In addition, it aligns with the goals of the sustainability team to promote environmental 
sustainability indicated below: It reduces plastic waste. The installation of water bottle filling stations will enable and 
encourage individuals to refill their reusable bottles instead of using single-use plastic bottles, which is beneficial for 
the environment. The installation of water bottle filling stations will be beneficial for both the users and the university 
in the long run. Individuals will be less inclined to purchase bottled water and save money while the university can lower 
the waste management costs associated with disposing of plastic waste and show their dedication to sustainability issues.
Finally, it will have a positive impact on the environment by promoting resource conservation.

What type of data will you collect or be analyzing?     
Most of the data is already collected by the Boston University Sustainability team.
We will be analyzing several data 
Locations of current water bottle filling stations (existing water filling stations): This data maps all the locations of existing 
water bottle filling stations on campus. To remove redundancy, our report will exclude these locations and these current stations 
might provide information on what aspects to prioritize in our project.

Wifi Data for foot traffic: This data shows the number of people connected to Boston University’s wifi at numerous locations. They 
will be examined to determine the locations that might benefit the most through the installation of new water bottle filling stations. 
The higher the WiFi usage is, the higher the demand for a station.

High-Volume Event Spaces: This data contains the specifics of locations and buildings used for high-volume event spaces. Areas that 
are frequently used for significant gatherings will require water bottle filling stations.

Building and Kitchen List: This data provides details on the buildings across the Charles Campus at Boston University. It will be 
used to determine whether the buildings have a common kitchen (do not require a water bottle filling station) and whether the 
buildings have air conditioning (prioritize installing water bottle filling station). More data if needed in the future

What are the potential limitations of the project?   

Water bottle filling stations require regular maintenance to ensure their cleanliness and proper filter functionality. Buildings that 
lack regular maintenance but require a water bottle fountain station can be a possible limitation. Although the client mentioned that 
we should not care about the budget, they emphasized installing water bottle filling stations near existing plumbings to minimize the 
cost. If the locations we select lack nearby plumbing, it will lead to higher expenses for the university. The dataset does not contain 
how many bottles each existing water bottle station has saved. This information would help determine the effectiveness of the existing 
stations, which will impact our decisions on additional locations to build water bottle filling stations. In addition, even if we can 
physically go around the buildings to check their numbers, some of the numbers are not indicated clearly. The client mentioned focusing 
on expanding water bottle filling stations within the Charles Campus, leaving out the Medical and Fenway Campus, which might also require 
water bottle filling stations. The client also mentioned focusing on installing water bottle-filling stations indoors, but some 
individuals might require a few outdoor stations.
